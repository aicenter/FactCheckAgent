# Verification Checklist

## Source Quality

- No citations from tier-5 sources
- At least one tier-1 source cited for the main factual claim
- No foreign-language sources when a Czech equivalent exists
- No opinion/commentary articles cited as factual sources
- No paywalled sources when a free alternative covers the same information
- No sources published after the claim date used as evidence

## Citation Format

- Every hyperlink is on a keyword or key phrase, not on a media outlet name
- No separate reference section — all citations are inline
- PDFs include page numbers; videos include timestamps; long documents reference specific sections
- At least 2-3 links spot-checked for correct content

## Text Structure and Length

- Explanation is between 3 and 6 paragraphs (excluding the summary)
- No paragraph exceeds 5 sentences
- No conclusion or fact appears twice; verdict stated once at the end
- Every paragraph contributes directly to the verdict — no tangential filler
- Trivially obvious facts are not given their own paragraphs

## Verdict and Reasoning

- Verdict is consistent with sub-claim evaluations
- Verdict is based on substance, not minor linguistic imprecisions
- If claim is ambiguous, the most charitable reasonable interpretation was evaluated
- No speculation about the speaker's intent
- No unsourced claims presented as fact

## Language Quality

- All foreign quotes translated to Czech
- Technical terms explained on first use
- Neutral tone — no advocacy or evaluative language
- Natural Czech — no robotic patterns, awkward declension, or repetitive structures
- Political roles current as of the claim date
- Political entities correctly identified as "strana" or "hnuti"

## Context and Introduction

- Context paragraph present if the claim requires it, without repeating the claim
- Opening is not dry or mechanical
- Short summary (max 280 characters) present at the beginning
