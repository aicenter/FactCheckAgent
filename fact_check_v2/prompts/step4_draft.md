# Step 4: Write the Draft

Recall these files before writing:
- `work/refined_sources.md` — your validated sources
- `work/claim_interpretation.md` — your analysis of the claim
- `guides/style_guide.md` — the Demagog style rules (read this carefully!)
- `guides/examples.txt` — real examples of properly formatted reports (read at least 2 to calibrate tone and style)

Write the fact-check report to `work/draft.md`.

## What to Write

Write a complete fact-check report in Czech following the style guide. The report must contain:

1. **Summary** (max 280 characters): The essence of the verdict. Not "hodnotime jako pravdu" but WHY.
2. **Report body** (3-6 paragraphs in HTML format, for very complex claims up to 8):
   - Context paragraph (if needed) — concise, adds information beyond the claim itself
   - Evidence paragraphs — each covering one sub-claim or group of evidence
   - Conclusion — confident, clear verdict stated once

## Critical Rules (from the style guide)

- Context paragraph may include what happened before the claim in the transcript, such as a question
- All hyperlinks go on KEYWORDS, never on media names
- The first time the speaker is mentioned, use their full name and role
- No separate reference section — all citations inline
- No bullet points, or lists in the body
- Use headers to indicate the content of (a group of) paragraphs 
- No bold text or capitals (except proper nouns)
- Simple short sentences, short paragraphs (max 5 sentences each)
- Translate all foreign quotes to Czech
- Use only sources from refined_sources.md — do not add new sources
- Do not verify trivially obvious facts
- Do not number sub-claims ("za prve, za druhe")
- Verdict must be one of: pravda, nepravda, zavadejici, neoveritelna
- The heading of the conclusion parahgraph is one of:
  - Jak jsme výrok ověřili? (for true)
  - Proč je výrok nepravdivý? (for false)
  - V čem je výrok zavádějící? (for misleading)
  - Proč se nám výrok nepodařilo ověřit? (for unverifiable)
- Verdict must be consistent with your evidence — if all sub-claims are true, do not use "zavadejici" without explaining a material omission

## Note on sources
Write primarily from the sources in refined_sources.md. If while writing you realize a sub-claim lacks adequate sourcing, go back: search for additional sources, update raw_sources.md and refined_sources.md, then continue the draft.
